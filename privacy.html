Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Updated for Phozzle 1.1 on {{PUBLISH_DATE}}

Summary

Phozzle has no servers of its own. We do not collect, store, share or sell any of your data. Photos are picked and analyzed on your iPhone. The only time anything leaves your iPhone is the optional AI captions feature, which is off until you agree to it.

Photo library

With your permission, Phozzle reads your photo library on your device to pick photos for puzzles. It only reads; it never changes or deletes anything in your library. All analysis of photos happens on your iPhone.

Data stored on your device

Photos you solve, their frames and your progress are saved in Phozzle's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting Phozzle deletes this data.

In-app purchase

The first 10 puzzles are free. The full version is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Phozzle never sees your payment details; it only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase is active on your device.

To keep the free count accurate after reinstalling, Phozzle stores a single number (how many puzzles you have solved) in your iPhone's keychain. It contains nothing else and never leaves your device.

Optional AI captions (Phozzle 1.1 and later)

When you open an Epic or Legendary frame, Phozzle can ask an AI to write a playful one-line caption for that photo. The first time, Phozzle asks you before doing anything. If you decline, Phozzle never connects.

If you agree, for each Epic or Legendary frame Phozzle sends, directly from your iPhone to DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com) over an encrypted connection:

  • one small copy of the framed photo (at most 512 pixels on its longest side), re-drawn so that it carries no location, camera or other metadata;
  • a short hint of the time of day and season it was taken (for example "late night, autumn"). No exact time, no place, no coordinates.

Nothing is sent through our servers, and we receive nothing. DeepSeek returns one line of text, which Phozzle keeps only on your iPhone, next to that photo. DeepSeek processes the request under its own privacy policy, published at deepseek.com. No account, name, device identifier or other personal information is sent; as with any internet connection, DeepSeek can see the network (IP) address the request comes from.

With captions on, Phozzle also shows where a photo was taken. The location comes from the photo's own metadata on your iPhone; to turn it into a city name, Phozzle asks Apple's map service (Apple's privacy policy applies). The location is never sent to DeepSeek.

You can turn AI captions off at any time with the switch at the top right of the Gallery. Turning them off stops all future requests; captions already written stay on your iPhone until you delete Phozzle.

No tracking

Apart from the optional AI captions above and Apple's own App Store purchase handling, Phozzle makes no network connections: no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Phozzle collects no data from anyone, including children. AI captions are off unless they are turned on in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
