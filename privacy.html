Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Summary

Phozzle does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any of your data. Your photos never leave your iPhone.

Photo library

With your permission, Phozzle reads your photo library on your device to pick photos for puzzles. It only reads; it never changes or deletes anything in your library. All analysis of photos happens on your iPhone.

Data stored on your device

Photos you solve, their frames and your progress are saved in Phozzle's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting Phozzle deletes this data.

No network, no tracking

Phozzle has no network features: no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Phozzle collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
