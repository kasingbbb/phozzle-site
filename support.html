Contact

For questions, bug reports or suggestions, open an issue at github.com/kasingbbb/phozzle-site/issues. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Phozzle says photo access is off.
Open Settings → Phozzle → Photos and allow access. Phozzle needs your photo library to pick photos for puzzles.

How do I solve a puzzle?
Drag one piece onto another and the two swap places. Put every piece back where it belongs before you run out of moves.

What does solving faster do?
The faster you solve, the better your odds of a rarer frame when you reveal the photo.

Is Phozzle free?
Your first 10 puzzles are free. After that, unlock the full version with a one-time purchase. It is not a subscription.

I bought the full version but it's locked again.
On the unlock screen, tap Restore Purchase and sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Phozzle itself makes no network connections. Your photos are processed on your iPhone and never leave it.

Where are my finished photos?
On your gallery wall inside Phozzle. They are stored only on this iPhone, so deleting the app deletes them.
